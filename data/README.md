The survey and simulation results are supplied separately. No data are included.

The simulation input is the consolidated, cleaned survey workbook used in the
study, not the original multi-sheet questionnaire. Preserve its respondent order
and standardized community-household identifiers. Pass its location with --data.

Survey field names are represented by Unicode escape sequences in the source
code. This preserves compatibility with the questionnaire without literal
Chinese characters in the repository.

Generated node attributes, recipient masks and network edges remain local
outputs. Do not upload them without the appropriate data-sharing approval.
